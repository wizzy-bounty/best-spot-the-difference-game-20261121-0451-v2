The hunt

Two pictures of the same scene hang side by side, and eight things differ between them. Click a difference in either picture to circle it in gold on both. Finds build your streak and your score; wrong guesses cost a strike, and three strikes — or an empty clock — end the scene. Finish a scene to unlock the next.

Mouse

  • Move to inspect — the crosshair mirrors on the other picture.
  • Click a difference to mark it.
  • Toggle the lens to magnify the spot under your cursor.
  • Spend a hint (−25 points) to circle one difference softly.

Keyboard

  • ←→↑↓ or WASD move the crosshair (hold Shift for speed)
  • Enter guess · Tab switch picture
  • H hint · L lens · P pause · R restart · N next scene
  • 1–5 jump to an unlocked scene · M mute
